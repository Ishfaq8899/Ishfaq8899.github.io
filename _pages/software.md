## R Packages

- **[MCEDsim](https://github.com/Ishfaq8899/MCEDsim)** <br>
  Simulation platform for multicancer early detection (MCED) testing. An R package for simulating the population-level benefits and harms of MCED testing, with pre-fitted natural history models calibrated to US SEER incidence data across 15 solid cancer sites and a flexible engine to project outcomes across test sensitivities, pre-clinical detectable windows, and screening strategies.

- **[Othercause](https://github.com/Ishfaq8899/Othercause)** <br>
  An R package for generating reproducible age-, sex-, and year-specific estimates of other-cause mortality for use in MCED life table analyses.
